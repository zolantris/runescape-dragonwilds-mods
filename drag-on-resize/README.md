# DragOnResize (v0.3.3)

Move and scale inventory windows across your full screen (including 21:9 and 32:9 ultrawide monitors) in **RuneScape: Dragonwilds** with 4-corner viewport clamping, persistent layouts, and third-party mod extensibility.

## Controls
- **Mouse Drag:** Hold **Middle Mouse** or **Alt + Left Mouse** (or tap **Alt + M** to pick up / drop)
- **Mouse Resize:** Hold **Alt + Right Mouse** and drag cursor (or tap **Alt + R** to toggle)
- **Keyboard Move (Hold-to-Repeat):** **Alt + Arrow Keys**
- **Keyboard Scale (Hold-to-Repeat):** **Alt + PageUp / PageDown** or **Alt + NumPad + / NumPad -**
- **Reset Active Panel:** **Alt + Backspace** or **Alt + NumPad 0**
- **Reset All Panels:** **Alt + Shift + Backspace**
